Highlighting

  • When on, highlighting runs everywhere except the excluded websites. When off, it only runs on the enabled websites. Excluded websites are never highlighted, in either mode. Edit the lists

Appearance

  • The colors of the popup and of this page. Auto is light or dark, as the browser is.

  • How many matches the page has, on the uHighlight icon. The popup and the results panel show them per word.

Shortcuts

  • Turn highlighting on or off for the current site

    Chrome keeps the keyboard shortcuts of extensions on a page of its own.

    not set
  • Highlight selected text

    Right-click selected text on any page and choose Highlight “…” to add it to your words. This also turns highlighting on for that site.

Backup

  • Export settings

    Saves your words, colors, options and website lists to a JSON file.

  • Import settings

    Shows what the file holds first: merge it with your settings, or replace them.

  • Clear all words

    Removes every word of My words (saved lists are kept, and so are your website settings). You can undo it right after.
